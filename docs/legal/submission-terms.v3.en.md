### 4. What may be published

Your submission remains private pending review. Choose one publication setting:

* **Record and abstract only:** the approved title, author names, affiliations, bibliographic details, author-written abstract and citation may be public. The full document remains private.
* **Record, abstract and full text:** the same information and the approved document may be public, with online reading and downloads for personal study and research.

Publication requires administrative approval, appropriate permissions and eligibility for the current public collection. We may request corrections, delay or decline publication, or restrict access where rights, privacy, record quality or service operation require it. A new institution becoming eligible does not automatically publish its submissions.

Broader permissions, replacement documents and new public uses must be recorded separately. Public AI-generated summaries or translations are not authorized by these settings.

### 6. Automated reading with Google Gemini (free tier)

**The default, and your choice.** After upload, the Platform can suggest your document's title, abstract, year, institution, faculty and degree using Google's Gemini API, an external AI service. Before you submit, you can choose to enter these details manually instead. If you do, nothing from your document is sent to Gemini. If automated reading does not work, you can switch to manual entry without uploading again.

**What is sent, and what is not.** We do not send your document to Gemini. Our server first reads the file itself and prepares a short excerpt from it: lines from the cover page that name the institution, faculty, degree and date, lines that appear to be the title, and the abstract section or sections. Before anything is sent, it removes lines and sentences that look like names of people or contact details, including the lines after labels such as "By", "Supervisor", "إعداد" and "إشراف"; lines with titles such as "Dr." or "Prof."; lines containing the names entered in the submission form; email and web addresses; phone, student and other identification numbers; and sentences that thank or credit people. Declarations, dedications, acknowledgements and the rest of the document are never sent. Only that excerpt, with our instructions, is sent, and only to suggest the details listed above. Gemini is never asked for the names of authors or supervisors: you enter those yourself. The contact details you enter in the submission form are not sent to Gemini.

**When nothing is sent.** If we cannot prepare such an excerpt reliably, nothing is sent and you enter the details yourself. This happens, for example, when the file is a scan without readable text, when its text cannot be read reliably, when too little remains once names and contact details are removed, or when the title or abstract may name a person.

**The removal is automatic and can miss things.** It follows fixed rules and is not guaranteed to find every name or personal detail. For example, a name that the rules do not recognise, printed without any label or title, may not be detected. Some titles cannot be told apart from a name; they are not sent, and you enter them yourself. Choose "Enter details manually" if your title or abstract contains personal, sensitive or confidential information, such as the names of people, information about identifiable research participants, health information or unpublished confidential results.

**Google's terms that apply.** The Platform uses the Gemini API under Google's terms for unpaid services (the free tier). Under those terms, Google uses the content submitted to the service and the responses it generates to provide, improve and develop Google's products and services and machine-learning technologies, consistent with Google's Privacy Policy. Human reviewers may read, annotate and process that input and output; Google states that it takes steps to protect privacy in this process, including disconnecting the data from the Google account, API key and Cloud project before reviewers see or annotate it. Google's terms do not state how long content used in these ways is kept.

**Google's abuse monitoring.** Separately, Google's abuse-monitoring policy for the Gemini API states that Google retains prompts, any context sent with them and the responses for 55 days to detect and prevent violations of its prohibited-use policy and for any required legal or regulatory disclosures; that authorised Google employees may assess content flagged for this purpose; and that data logged for this purpose is used only for policy enforcement. The 55-day period applies to these abuse-monitoring records only, not to content used to improve Google's products.

**Where Google processes data.** Google's Privacy Policy states that Google maintains servers around the world and that information may be processed on servers located outside the country where you live. We do not control what Google keeps or where, and withdrawing or deleting your submission from the Platform does not delete anything Google holds. Google's terms may change; this section reflects Google's Gemini API Additional Terms of Service (last updated 28 April 2026), its Gemini API abuse-monitoring policy (last updated 9 June 2026) and its Privacy Policy (effective 1 October 2026).

**Our record of what was sent.** We keep the excerpt that was sent, and the suggestions returned, with your submission's processing record, so that what was sent can be checked later. It is not shown on any page of the Platform; only the Platform's server, and the operator through the database, can read it. It is kept for as long as your submission's records are kept and is deleted with them (section 8).

**What your acceptance can and cannot do.** Google asks that sensitive, confidential or personal information not be submitted to its unpaid services. Your acceptance does not change that restriction, and it cannot authorize the use of anyone else's personal information, including that of your coauthors, your supervisors or anyone else named in the document. That is why only the excerpt described above is sent, why nothing is sent when it cannot be prepared, and why manual entry is always available.

**Check every suggestion.** Suggested details can be wrong or incomplete. Check and correct every detail before you confirm. What you confirm is what we record. Publication decisions remain with authorized reviewers.

**Other uses.** We do not sell private documents or personal contact information. The Platform does not itself use submitted documents to train AI models. Google's use of an excerpt sent to Gemini is governed by Google's terms, as described above. The Platform's own automated checks may compare bibliographic details to detect duplicate deposits; these checks do not send documents outside the Platform. This agreement does not authorize use of private documents in a cross-institution plagiarism service or a student-facing AI research-direction service. Such uses require a separate rights and privacy process before introduction.

### 7. Privacy

We use submission details, documents, contact information, acceptance records, correspondence and limited technical and usage information to administer submissions, provide the agreed services, protect the Platform and address requests or legal obligations.

Approved research information may be public within your selected setting. Submission contact details, private management links, unpublished files and internal review notes are not public profile fields. An optional LinkedIn link is public only if you choose to display it.

Service providers include Vercel for hosting the website and its server functions, Supabase for database and document storage, and Google, through the Gemini API, for automated reading of a minimized excerpt as described in section 6 unless you choose manual entry. Messages sent to our Gmail address are handled through Google's email service. Relevant information is processed outside Sudan: our database and document storage are currently hosted in Frankfurt, Germany, and Google states that it maintains servers around the world and may process information on servers located outside the country where you live. Provider access is limited to what the service requires, subject to applicable contractual arrangements; security logs and legally required disclosures may still occur.

Authorized administrators and volunteers may access information needed for their duties. Public activity statistics are aggregated and do not identify individual readers. A view, download or citation export does not establish that the work was read or cited. Optional alerts and promotional messages require a separate choice where applicable.

### 8. Retention, correction and withdrawal

Research records are retained while they serve the authorized repository purpose. Other personal information is retained only as needed to administer the submission, resolve requests, protect the service or meet legal obligations. Unneeded temporary files and operational records are deleted or anonymized. Retention is reviewed when a submission is abandoned, rejected, withdrawn or otherwise no longer needed.

Contact sarpcontact2026@gmail.com to request access, correction, restriction, withdrawal or deletion. We may ask for proportionate evidence of identity or authority. On an accepted withdrawal, we stop the affected public access and remove the material from our active search systems. A minimal withdrawal notice may remain where lawful and appropriate, without exposing sensitive information.

Limited acceptance, rights or complaint records may be retained where justified. Residual backup copies may remain until overwritten or deleted through the applicable backup process; they must not be used to restore withdrawn public access. We cannot remove copies held independently by others or revoke rights already granted under an irrevocable licence.

We will exercise reasonable care, but do not guarantee uninterrupted service, indefinite preservation or error-free content. Nothing in this agreement excludes responsibility or rights that applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

Material changes will be identified by version. They do not expand permissions for earlier submissions without the necessary new authorization. Your submission record identifies the terms and publication setting you accepted.
