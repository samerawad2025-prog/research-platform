# Sudanese Academic Research Platform

## Volunteer Confidentiality Commitment

**Version:** Draft 1  
**Date:** 29 SEP 2026  
**Status:** Draft for the founder's review. It is not in effect until the founder approves the wording and activates a version.  
**Contact:** sarpcontact2026@gmail.com

### 1. Why you are seeing this

As a volunteer reviewer you may be given access to research that has been submitted to the Platform and is not yet public, and to review information about it. You have this access only to help review the submissions assigned to you.

### 2. What you agree to

* Keep every submission you can see confidential. Do not share, forward, copy, publish or discuss it with anyone who is not authorized to review it.
* Use what you see only for the review you were asked to do. Do not use it for your own research, coursework, business or any other purpose.
* Open only the submissions assigned to you. Do not try to reach anything else.
* Download or keep a document only as far as your review needs it, and delete it afterwards.
* If you come across contact details, signatures, identity documents or participant information, do not share them, and tell the administrator.
* Your recommendations are advice. Publication decisions belong to the administrators, and you must not tell a submitter or anyone else that a submission has been approved or declined.

### 3. If something goes wrong

Tell sarpcontact2026@gmail.com as soon as you can if you think a submission has been seen by someone it should not have been, or if you have made a mistake.

### 4. How long this lasts

These commitments continue after you stop volunteering.

### 5. Your access is recorded

The Platform records that you acknowledged this text, which version, and when. It also records which files you open.

### Acknowledgement

**I have read and agree to keep the submissions I review confidential as described above.**
