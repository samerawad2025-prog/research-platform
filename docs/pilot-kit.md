# Phase 4 M2: small researcher pilot

Prepared 2026-10-20. Status: materials ready; recruitment and participant sessions have not started. These are proposed operating targets, not measured results or external benchmarks.

## Start here

1. Founder selects five willing University of Khartoum participants from existing contacts. Aim for three authors with completed papers and two readers, including a supervisor or postgraduate researcher if available. Include both interface languages and at least two phone users where feasible. Do not collect documents merely to meet a quota.
2. Send the appropriate [invitation](pilot-invitations.md) personally. No invitations have been sent by the platform or assistant. No university endorsement is implied.
3. Run the three tasks below. Budget about 20 minutes, but stop whenever the participant wants. Participation and submission are optional; a reader need not upload anything.
4. Record outcomes in a private cloud document using the blank template below. Keep completed notes outside this public repository.
5. Review the first five sessions, fix recurring friction, then invite a second small group. Expand toward 10–20 genuine, permission-reviewed records only when the workflow is usable.

Use the live site for genuine deposits only:
- Submit: https://research-platform-5zpu.vercel.app/submit
- Browse: https://research-platform-5zpu.vercel.app/research

For demonstrations, use the existing public record. Do not create synthetic papers in Production or republish old test deposits.

## Session script

Say: “We are testing the website, not you. Please tell me what you expect and where you get stuck. You can stop or skip any task. I will write brief notes without recording your screen or password.”

Arabic: «نحن نختبر الموقع، وليس قدراتك. أخبرني بما تتوقع حدوثه وأين تواجه صعوبة. يمكنك التوقف أو تجاوز أي مهمة. سأكتب ملاحظات مختصرة دون تسجيل الشاشة أو كلمة المرور.»

Do not coach until the participant asks or cannot proceed. Record help given. Never request their private confirmation link or credentials. If a private link is displayed during an author-led demonstration, do not copy it into notes.

### Task 1: submit and confirm (authors only)

“Submit a paper you are entitled to deposit, check the suggested details, correct anything inaccurate and confirm.”

«أرسل بحثاً تملك صلاحية إيداعه، وراجع البيانات المقترحة وصحح الأخطاء ثم أكدها.»

The author chooses the permission and processing options themselves. Do not check acceptance boxes for them or describe acceptance as a formality. Automatic Gemini extraction remains the default; manual entry is available. Use the actual on-screen explanation and terms. A decision not to upload is useful feedback, not a failed participant.

Observe: finding the form, understanding the choices, upload progress, extraction, corrections, confirmation and understanding that publication requires review. Record extraction corrections by field category only, not private document content. Stop on wrong-document exposure, loss of data or an unexpected acceptance/publication state; report the problem before another deposit.

### Task 2: find a paper (all participants)

“Find a paper relevant to your interests. If none is available, find the current published paper and tell me what you can learn from its record.”

«ابحث عن بحث يرتبط باهتماماتك. إذا لم تجد، افتح البحث المنشور حالياً وأخبرني بما فهمته من صفحته.»

Record catalogue navigation, search/filter confusion and whether the participant can identify the authors, institution and year. Separate “no relevant content in this small collection” from “could not use search”. Do not imply that a one-record collection proves discovery quality.

### Task 3: cite and access (all participants)

“Get a citation for that paper, then find how to read its document. Explain what you think you may reuse.”

«احصل على صيغة للاستشهاد بالبحث، ثم اعثر على طريقة قراءة ملفه. وضح ما تعتقد أنه يمكنك إعادة استخدامه.»

Check that the participant can identify a citation export and the reading/download controls. A successful export is not an academic citation. They may skip a download because of data cost or connectivity; record that separately from a broken feature.

Finish with:
- What was hardest? / ما أصعب خطوة؟
- What would make you use this again? / ما الذي يشجعك على استخدامه مرة أخرى؟
- What seemed unclear or untrustworthy? / ما الذي بدا غير واضح أو أثار شكوكك؟

## Founder publication checklist

Use the existing admin workflow for every genuine deposit:
- Confirmed metadata, appropriate document type, author names, title, year and abstract.
- Institution resolved correctly. Launch publication remains limited to eligible UofK records.
- Recorded permission and depositor authority support the intended publication scope.
- Possible duplicates inspected manually; a similar title is not proof of plagiarism.
- Whole dissemination document reviewed before full-text approval; redact where necessary.
- Blocking issues, embargo and any applicable restriction resolved.
- Choose Approve for publication and the reviewed version when needed. Verify the public page after saving.

Do not publish merely to improve pilot numbers. A hold or decline may be the correct outcome. Record reasons privately in the admin system. Founder time is operational workload, not a cash payment.

## Weekly scorecard

Report counts with denominators, even when small. Do not put participant-level results in GitHub.

| Measure | How to count |
|---|---|
| Participation | Invited, agreed, completed; do not treat silence as agreement |
| Submission completion | Confirmed genuine deposits / genuine deposits started in this cohort |
| Review outcome | Approved, needs changes, pending, declined, withdrawn for this cohort |
| Backlog | Confirmed cohort deposits awaiting a decision; age of oldest |
| Review workload | Median active founder minutes per reviewed record, plus range and sample size |
| Usability | Independent, assisted and incomplete outcomes for each attempted task |
| Content coverage | Relevant record found vs no relevant content available |
| Processing quality | Field categories corrected and extraction failures, with counts |
| Platform activity | Existing page/document/export counts; these include non-pilot activity and are not unique readers or academic citations |

The platform does not currently measure abandoned forms or per-task timings. Record observed starts and timings manually; label unobserved totals unknown. Do not invent a conversion rate from stored submissions alone. Allow for papers started in one week and confirmed in another; report by cohort and snapshot date.

## Decision after five sessions

Proposed practical gate, not statistical validation:
- At least four participants attempt discovery and citation tasks; at least three eligible authors attempt submission. If fewer attempt, report insufficient evidence and recruit more willing participants.
- At least 80% complete each attempted task without founder intervention. With three author attempts, this means all three; always show the raw counts.
- Any repeated blocker affecting two participants gets a fix or documented workaround before expanding recruitment.
- Any unintended publication, private-data exposure or lost submission pauses the affected workflow until investigated.
- Report content scarcity separately. Use it to guide the next collection, not as evidence the search code is broken.

M2 is complete only after real sessions, a summarized findings review, and resolution or explicit acceptance of important issues. A prepared kit alone does not complete M2. “Explore research directions” remains M3; no new AI provider, automated invitations or volunteer accounts are part of this pilot.
